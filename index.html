About me

Hi, I'm Noah

I grew up in Winnipeg, Canada, and I believe books should be free for everyone — I'm trying my hardest to make that happen.

When I'm not writing, I'm playing floor hockey or baseball, travelling (22 countries and counting), or thinking about the universe. I also help other authors publish their books.
