Bio

Noah Lieberman grew up in Winnipeg, Canada. He started writing while in business school, when he noticed that no one had done a deep dive into the fantasy sports industry, and felt it was desperately needed.

He graduated from the University of Manitoba in 2022. His passions include sports, travel, philosophy, and studying the universe. He plays floor hockey in the winter, and baseball in the summer. He's also been to 25 countries!

Noah has published four books so far, and has plans to write more. He has also worked with other authors to publish their books successfully.

He loves the idea that books (like other content these days) can become free through non-intrusive, relevant advertising. Breaking down the price barrier, and providing relevant businesses with a new avenue to reach customers.

In his day-to-day, Noah serves as Chief of Staff to the CEO at Taiv, an AI startup in the advertising and media sector.

“Books should be free for everyone.”
