BIO

Noah Lieberman grew up in Winnipeg, Canada. He believes that books should be free for everyone, and is trying his hardest to make that happen.

Noah graduated from the University of Manitoba in 2022. His passions include sports, travel, philosophy, and studying the universe. He plays floor hockey in the winter, and baseball in the summer. He's also been to 22 countries!

Noah has published four books so far, and has plans to write more. He also works with other authors to publish their books successfully.
