Questions

Do I get to approve the ad read?

Yes. I'll draft it and send it to you before anything is published, and we'll go back and forth until you're happy with it.

Is the whole book about my company?

No — the book is my writing, start to finish. Your brand appears on the cover and in the preface. That keeps the book genuinely worth reading, which is what makes the sponsorship valuable.

How is the book distributed?

I give it away free on my website and other channels. We can also distribute it through yours — your newsletter, customers, or events.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the book and the scope of the partnership. Send me a note below and I'll share options.

Can a book have more than one sponsor?

Each sponsored edition is built around a single partner, so your brand isn't competing for attention.
