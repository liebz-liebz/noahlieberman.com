How it works

Three steps from handshake to readers

01

Your logo on the cover

Your brand appears on the front cover of the sponsored edition — the first thing every reader sees, and the image shared wherever the book is promoted.

02

An ad read in the preface

I write a short, genuine message about your company and place it in the preface, in my own voice — like a podcast host read, but in print. You approve it before it goes out.

03

Given away free

The ebook is distributed at no cost to readers. With no price tag in the way, more people download, read, and pass it along — and your brand travels with it.

Why sponsor a book

Attention that lasts longer than a scroll.

A banner ad gets a glance. A book gets hours. Sponsoring a free edition puts your brand alongside something people chose to spend time with.

  • Goodwill built inReaders know they're reading for free because of you.
  • Shareable by designA free book gets forwarded, posted, and recommended — your logo goes along for the ride.

FAQ

Common questions

Do I get to approve the ad read?

Yes. I'll draft it and send it to you before anything is published. We'll go back and forth until you're happy with it.

Is the whole book about my company?

No — the book is my writing, start to finish. Your brand appears on the cover and in the preface ad read. That keeps the book genuinely worth reading, which is what makes the sponsorship valuable.

How is the book distributed?

The ebook is given away free on my website and other channels. We can also talk about distributing it through your own channels — your newsletter, customers, or events.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the book and the scope of the partnership. Send me a note below and I'll share options.

Can one book have more than one sponsor?

Each sponsored edition is built around a single partner, so your brand isn't competing for attention.
